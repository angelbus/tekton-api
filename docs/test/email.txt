Hi team,

First of all, great work on the MVP2 release — this is a significant step forward both technically (100% serverless, event-driven, shared storage) and functionally (FMTM in place).

I noticed the repositories have been tagged as **1.1.0**, and I’d like to suggest aligning our versioning strategy with the scope of the changes delivered.

From an architectural and product perspective, MVP2 introduces:

* A fundamental shift in architecture (not incremental)
* New processing model and execution semantics
* New functional capability (FMTM vs Calibration-only in MVP1)

Given this, the release would more accurately correspond to a **major version (2.0.0)** under semantic versioning, as it represents a breaking and transformative change rather than a minor evolution.

Additionally, I strongly recommend:

* Maintaining a **Changelog** per repo (clear visibility of changes, impacts, and migration notes)
* Establishing a **mapping between MVPs and semantic versions** (e.g., MVP1 → 1.x, MVP2 → 2.x) to keep technical and business milestones aligned

This will improve clarity for:

* Internal teams (development, operations, architecture)
* Future integrations
* Governance and release tracking

Happy to help define a simple versioning + changelog standard if useful.

Thanks,
